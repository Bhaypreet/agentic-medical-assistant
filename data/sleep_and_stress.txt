Sleep and Stress Management for Overall Health

Sleep:
- Adults need 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night for optimal health.
- Poor sleep is linked to worse blood sugar control, higher blood pressure,
  weakened immunity, weight gain, and mood problems.
- Good sleep hygiene practices:
  - Keep a consistent sleep/wake schedule, even on weekends
  - Avoid screens (phones, TVs) for 30-60 minutes before bed
  - Keep the bedroom cool, dark, and quiet
  - Avoid caffeine after early afternoon and heavy meals close to bedtime
  - Limit daytime naps to 20-30 minutes

Stress:
- Chronic stress raises cortisol, which can worsen blood sugar control,
  blood pressure, and inflammation over time.
- Effective stress-management techniques:
  - Deep breathing exercises (e.g. 4-7-8 breathing: inhale 4s, hold 7s,
    exhale 8s)
  - Regular physical activity, even short walks
  - Mindfulness or meditation, 5-10 minutes daily
  - Maintaining social connections and talking through stressors
  - Setting realistic boundaries around work and commitments

When to Seek Help:
- Persistent difficulty sleeping (insomnia) lasting more than a few weeks
- Stress or anxiety that interferes with daily functioning
- Feelings of hopelessness, persistent low mood, or loss of interest in
  activities - these warrant a conversation with a healthcare provider or
  mental health professional.
